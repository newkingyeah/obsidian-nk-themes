# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-20

- Initial public release of Aurora Glass, Abyssal Bloom, and Neon Circuit.
- Aurora Glass exposes the animated aurora through frosted translucent editor panels while preserving its mint/lavender palette.
- Abyssal Bloom uses lighter, less saturated blue-gray surfaces in both modes.
- All themes include reduced-motion support and narrow-window adaptations.
